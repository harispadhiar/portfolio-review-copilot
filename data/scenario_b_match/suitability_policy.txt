Internal Suitability Policy - Retail Advisory Division
Document ref: SUIT-POL-07 (Rev. 4)

Purpose: This policy governs how advisors match recommended products to client risk profiles.

Section 3 - Risk Tolerance Matching
Advisors must not recommend a product whose risk category exceeds the client's documented
risk tolerance by more than one level on the firm's five-tier scale
(Conservative, Moderately Conservative, Balanced, Growth, Aggressive Growth).

Section 4 - Horizon Matching
Products recommended to clients with an investment horizon under 5 years must have a
recommended minimum holding period at or below the client's stated horizon. Equity-heavy
products with a stated 5+ year minimum horizon should not be recommended to clients with
liquidity needs inside that window.

Section 5 - Capital Loss Tolerance
Where a client has documented an explicit maximum acceptable annual capital loss, the
recommended product's historical worst-case drawdown must be disclosed to the client and
flagged in the advisory file if it exceeds that stated tolerance.

Section 6 - Documentation
Every recommendation must be accompanied by an internal advisory note explaining the
rationale and confirming the above checks were performed.
