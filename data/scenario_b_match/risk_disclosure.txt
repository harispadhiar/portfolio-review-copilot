Risk Disclosure Document - Balanced Growth Allocation Fund
This fund invests in a mix of equities and fixed-income instruments and is subject to
moderate market volatility, lower than a pure equity fund but higher than a money market
or income fund.
Investors should be prepared to accept short-term fluctuations in value in exchange for
long-term growth potential.
The fund is suitable for investors with a medium-to-long investment horizon and a
balanced or moderate risk tolerance.
Past performance is not indicative of future results.
